Architecture & governance

A five-layer pipeline where identity stays at the edge and only proofs, risk tiers and consented tasks travel.

L1

Vision AI

On-device OCR + biometric liveness extract coded lab values (LOINC). The raw image never leaves the device.

L2

ZK Privacy Vault

Salted commitment and ZK-ID alias. Proves "HbA1c ≥ 6.5%" without revealing who or the document.

L3

OPA Risk Router

Policy-as-code (Rego) routes by risk tier, consent and jurisdiction. Every decision is auditable.

L4

HITL Clinician Review

Coordinators see XAI factors and approve, remind or escalate. Decision support only.

L5

Closed-Loop FHIR Sync

FHIR R4 ServiceRequest/Task status returns to the loop until care is confirmed complete.
